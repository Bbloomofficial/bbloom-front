Let clients edit by clicking the page itself

Editing through a list of labelled inputs asks the client to hold a map in
their head: which of the eleven text boxes is the heading they can see. So both
editors gain an edit/review switch. In edit mode every value that can be
changed is marked on the preview, and clicking one selects its section and
focuses its field.

The site renderers are untouched. They are the components visitors are served,
and threading editor wiring through all of them would put an editing concern
into the public product, so values are matched back onto the DOM after render.
A value that cannot be matched simply gets no hotspot and stays editable in the
panel - a better failure than a preview that lies.

Pictures are no longer uploaded the moment they are picked, which made the
media library a record of everything a client ever considered. A pick is held
locally, shown from a blob URL and marked "not uploaded yet"; the upload
happens on save. A file or an image link both work, and a link is fetched into
a real file rather than kept as someone else's URL.

Saving also deletes the picture that was replaced, so a client swapping a photo
ten times does not leave ten behind - but only after checking every other
section, because the same logo legitimately appears in the header and the
footer.

Verified against production with the probe account: 76 hotspots marked, click
focused the right field, a deferred picture uploaded on save and its
predecessor was gone from the media library afterwards.
